  # About:
  This mod, like other horror mods, adds danger, how to kill the player. But I assure you that you will not be bored! I experimented with the style of entities and try to recreate the feeling that something is wrong.

  Vanilla mobs are gone to create a feeling of _isolation_. **Random events** can happen at any time _(disappearance of light sources, appearance of a sign with text, etc.)_. Fog that limits the player's vision.

  The squareness of the mob would ruin the atmosphere. The distinctive feature of the mod from all others is the models of entities. With the creation of mobs of the png type, you can create more details in the texture of the creature.

  Please suggest your ideas in issues. I'll be glad to read them.
  # Creatures:
